<h1 align="center">Code of Conduct</h1>

<br/>

Hydrodactyl is a project of the [Blueprint Framework](https://blueprint.zip) and is governed by the Blueprint community code of conduct.

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. All reports will be reviewed and investigated, and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances.

## Where to find it

The full code of conduct and enforcement guidelines are available at **[blueprint.zip/legal/conduct](https://blueprint.zip/legal/conduct)**.
